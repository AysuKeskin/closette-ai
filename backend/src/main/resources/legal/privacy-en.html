Closette privacy policy

Effective: 21 September 2026. Operator: {{operator}}. Contact: {{email}}.

What we collect and why

We store your email, username, optional display name, password hash, uploaded clothing and beauty photos, product details, wardrobe, saved outfits, reactions and style preferences to provide your account, inventory and recommendations. Authentication tokens and language preferences are stored securely on your device. We process IP addresses and request metadata to limit abuse and diagnose service failures. We do not use your inventory for advertising or sell it.

AI processing and your choice

With your permission, photos and descriptions you submit, relevant wardrobe items and style preferences are sent to {{provider}} to recognise items, read ingredient lists and produce outfit or shopping suggestions. Your password and authentication tokens are not sent to that provider. {{processing}}

Provider policy: {{providerUrl}}. Closette asks before your first external AI request and again when this disclosure changes. You can decline and browse your collection, or withdraw permission in Profile. Withdrawal stops subsequent requests; it cannot undo completed processing. Contact us for requests concerning data already processed.

Other processors

{{infrastructure}}. Transactional emails are delivered through our configured email provider. Product searches send the search words or barcode to Makeup API, Open Beauty Facts and, when enabled, the configured RapidAPI catalogue. Loading catalogue images connects your device to their image hosts, which receive your IP address. No advertising or tracking SDK is integrated in this release.

Storage and deletion

Uploaded photos are private and displayed through temporary signed links. Unsaved uploads are scheduled for deletion after 24 hours. Deleting an item queues its photo for deletion once no other item in your account references it. Deleting your account in Profile removes account records and queues all your tracked photos for deletion, including unsaved uploads. The cleanup worker normally runs every minute and retries storage failures; outages can delay completion. Legacy orphan uploads are reconciled daily. Restricted backup copies expire within {{backupDays}} days under our configured retention policy; restored backups must have deletion requests reapplied before service resumes. Third-party AI retention is described above.

Your rights and contact

You can edit your profile and inventory, withdraw AI permission and delete your account in the app. For access, correction, export, deletion or questions about applicable privacy rights and international processing, email {{email}}. We may verify your identity before fulfilling a request. We will describe any legally required retention when applicable.

Changes

We update this policy when processing changes. Material changes to external AI processing require renewed permission before further AI requests.
